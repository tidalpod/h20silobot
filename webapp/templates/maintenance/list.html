How Maintenance Works

Auto SMS to Vendors

Assigning a vendor automatically sends them an SMS with work order details, priority, and a link to their portal.

Status Workflow

Work orders flow through New, Assigned, In Progress, Completed, and Closed. Assigning a vendor auto-sets status to Assigned.

Vendor Portal

Vendors see assigned work orders in their portal, can view details, upload photos, and submit invoices directly.

Priority Levels

Tag work orders as Emergency, High, Normal, or Low priority to triage and filter your most urgent repairs.
