How Showings Work

Auto SMS to Vendors

When you assign a vendor to a showing, they automatically receive an SMS with the property, date, time, and a link to their vendor portal.

Auto SMS to Renters

If you add a prospective renter's phone number, they'll get an automatic text with the showing details and property address.

Vendor Portal Access

Assigned vendors can view their showings, confirm attendance, and see renter contact info directly from their portal.

Showing Types

Tag showings as Tenant, Inspector, Contractor, or Other to keep things organized and filter by type.
